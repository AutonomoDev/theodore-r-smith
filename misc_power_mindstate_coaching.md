## **Power Mind State Coaching (Colombia) — Humanitarian Nonprofit Initiative**

* **What it is:** A philanthropic coaching and rehabilitation project founded by Theodore in Colombia to help men — particularly those from the United States — overcome phobias and deep-seated anxieties around interacting with women.
* **Why it matters:** Many men suffer from social paralysis and fear-based isolation caused by cultural conditioning and trauma. Power Mind State Coaching provides a safe, structured, and ethical environment where they can relearn healthy communication and emotional confidence through immersive learning and guided empathy training.
* **Impact:** The program has helped participants rediscover self-worth, emotional intelligence, and respect-based confidence — transforming human connection into an act of mutual healing rather than performance.
* **Sound bite:** *“Restoring the confidence to connect — one conversation at a time.”*
