# Seeing Leaves For The First Time

As a child, I always drew trees as big circles of green, and a wise child psychologist recognized this as a true recounting of what i saw. extreme myopia. I only painted brown leaves because i only ever saw the ones that fell from trees, already dead. This lead to being diagnosed with very poor eyesight early.

I was very hesitatant and reluctant to wear glasses. Before the thin lenses, I had to wear extremely thick lenses, about 1 centimeter, and my peers would bully me relentlessly, even break the glasses. And they were quite heavy.

One day when I was 8 years old on the playground, my 2nd Grade teacher popped up behind me and threw my glasses right over my eyes as I was looking at the tree line.

That was the first moment I ever saw leaves on trees, and I remember it quite vividly. I decided to wear glasses from that day.

When I was 32 years old, i underwent LASIK and the surgeon told me it was a miracle, like hitting a field goal from the 90 yard line, but it was an extremely successful surgery and i've never worn glasses since, out of sheer appreciation. 12 years later, still going strong.
