## 4) Flagship Projects

### Autonomo (Autónomo) — Autonomous Programming Platform

* **What it is:** A platform where human engineers and LLM agents co-create and self-repair code. Agentic pipelines draft, review, test, and evolve autonomously.
* **Why it matters:** Turns software development into a *self-healing ecosystem*. Enables **sovereign, on-prem LLM** deployments for public-sector and enterprise.
* **Sound bite:** *“From prompt to production — with ethics, provenance, and resilience.”*

### Mycelium — Autonomous Self-Healing System

* **What it is:** A distributed orchestration layer that monitors runtime behavior, predicts failure, and automatically restores service continuity — inspired by biological mycelial networks.
* **Impact:** Merges AI-driven diagnosis with deterministic recovery. Inspired by biology’s immune systems and fungal communication networks.
* **Sound bite:** *“When your infrastructure heals itself, uptime becomes philosophy.”*

### BetterGist — Composer Supply-Chain Shield (2018–Present)

* **What it is:** A continuous archiver that **mirrors every PHP Composer package**, preserving canonical versions.
* **Impact:** Creates a failsafe against registry hijacks and stealthy dependency swaps; one reason **PHP has avoided ecosystem-wide supply-chain attacks**.
* **Sound bite:** *“The invisible backbone keeping PHP boring — and safe.”*

### BitBasket — Early Crypto Innovation (2010–2018)

* **What it is:** One of the earliest self-custody crypto portfolio managers — effectively a personal ETF for multi-coin collections.
* **Impact:** Allowed users to manage diversified crypto portfolios locally, long before DeFi wallets existed.
* **Sound bite:** *“Before DeFi had a name, BitBasket made it personal.”*

### Dockerize PHP — Zero-Friction Containerization

* **What it is:** A drop-in Docker toolbox supporting PHP 5.6→8.x, Nginx, Redis, PostgreSQL, MariaDB, Oracle extensions.
* **Impact:** **99.999%** compatibility across **350k+** packages; lets teams containerize legacy and modern apps identically.
* **Sound bite:** *“Your PHP, containerized in minutes — not sprints.”*

### BashScripts — The Swiss Army of Unix QoL

* **What it is:** Hundreds of self-contained scripts; no installer; predictable flags; safe defaults.
* **Impact:** Used by **~10,000** developers; a shared grammar of automation.
* **Sound bite:** *“Where Linux muscle meets human empathy — hundreds of one-liners that make the terminal feel alive.”*

### Power Mind State Coaching (Colombia) — Humanitarian Nonprofit Initiative

* **What it is:** A philanthropic project founded to help men overcome phobias and social anxiety around interacting with women, using immersive learning and guided empathy coaching.
* **Why it matters:** Many men struggle with cultural and emotional barriers that hinder authentic human connection. The program offers a safe, structured environment to relearn communication, confidence, and mutual respect.
* **Sound bite:** *“Restoring the confidence to connect — one conversation at a time.”*

### xMule (2003–2008)

* **What it is:** Cross-platform C++/wxWidgets P2P client — Linux-first, stability-driven.
* **Impact:** Battle-tested network code, portable UI/tooling, and lessons in resilience against hostile networks.
* **Sound bite:** *“Where Theodore learned to engineer for chaos.”*
