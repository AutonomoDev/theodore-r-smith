- **Quest for depth:**  A child who cannot trust the surface obsessively pursues hidden structure and truth—an origin story for his systems‑thinking mindset.

Theodore’s 54 % introvert / 46 % extrovert split is a rare blend. Introversion forms his core “charging dock,” while extroversion is an executive tool he calls on when needed.
This duality explains how he can lead a room or a company yet still prefer the quiet where ideas crystallize. It also lets him oscillate effortlessly between **deep, isolated coding** and **public leadership** while staying true to himself.
